### Python Challenge #N

```python
x = 9
y = x
x = 7
print (y)
```

**Answers**

- a) 7
- b) 9
- c) Error

<details>
<summary>Show Answer</summary>

**Correct answer: B**

Short explanation of why:

When you write y = x, y gets the value 9 at that moment.
Reassigning x = 7 only changes what x points to. y isn't linked to x, so it stays 9
